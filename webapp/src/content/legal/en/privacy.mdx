## 2. What we collect, and why

We collect very little. There is no advertising, no behavioural tracking and no third-party analytics on this website.

**When you submit the contact form** we receive:

- the name and email address you provide;
- the organisation and role you provide, if any;
- the subject and message you write;
- a one-way hashed version of your IP address (the original IP cannot be recovered);
- a truncated version of your browser's User-Agent string.

We use this information for the sole purpose of replying to your enquiry and, where relevant, scoping a possible engagement.

The legal basis for this processing is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Art. 6(6) revDSG) together with our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 31(1) revDSG).

**When you visit the website** the server logs ordinary HTTP request information (timestamp, requested URL, response code) for a short period for security and stability purposes. We do not associate these logs with form submissions or with any tracking identifier.

## 3. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal data and we do not use it for marketing.

Limited recipients:

- **Google Workspace** processes contact-form notifications and any subsequent email correspondence as our processor, under standard contractual terms.
- **Amazon Web Services (Lightsail)** hosts the application and database as our processor.

We do not transfer your data to any other recipient unless required by law.

## 4. International transfers

Our hosting region is the European Union. Google Workspace may process email content outside the EEA; Google maintains standard contractual clauses and certifications for these transfers.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Contact-form submissions: **24 months** from the date submitted, unless we are in active correspondence with you about a current or prospective engagement.
- Server access logs: **30 days**.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG), you have the right to:

- access the data we hold about you;
- rectify inaccurate data;
- request erasure of your data;
- restrict processing in certain circumstances;
- object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, write to **privacy@executivefounders.com**. We will respond within 30 days.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority:

- **Switzerland** — the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), [www.edoeb.admin.ch](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch).
- **European Union** — the supervisory authority of your member state.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses **only essential resources** to function. We do not set cookies for analytics, advertising or behavioural tracking.

Two technical details worth noting:

- **Local storage.** Your browser stores a small "consent acknowledged" record once you interact with the cookie notice, so we do not show the notice on every visit. You can clear it from your browser settings, or via **Cookie preferences** in the footer.
- **Google Fonts.** We load the Inter typeface from `fonts.googleapis.com`. This causes your browser to make a request to Google, which receives your IP address as part of the normal request. No cookies are set on our domain.

If we add any cookies in the future, this notice will be updated and we will request your consent before non-essential cookies are placed on your device.

## 8. Security

We use TLS for all traffic to and from this website. Submitted form data is stored in a hardened database accessible only to the application. We do not handle or retain payment information on this site.
