We help organisations meet their obligations under the emerging AI governance landscape — designing the controls, documentation and accountability structures required for compliance with **ISO/IEC 42001** (AI Management Systems), the **EU AI Act** (risk-tiered obligations) and the **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**. Work typically combines a readiness assessment, a mapping of existing controls to each framework, and the implementation of the missing controls in a form the organisation can actually live with — not a binder, a working operating model.
