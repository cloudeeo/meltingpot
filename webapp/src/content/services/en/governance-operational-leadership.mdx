We help organisations design governance that matches the speed and complexity of the business — decision rights, escalation paths, the cadence of steering — and we provide interim operational leadership where the role isn't yet in place. The objective is consistent: turn the unspoken into something written down, agreed and used. Engagements are medium-term because governance has to be lived in for at least a quarter before it settles.
