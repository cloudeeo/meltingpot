We design and stand up strategic PMOs and programme governance layers that make portfolio progress visible, dependencies explicit, and decisions reachable in a single meeting. The work focuses on the seams between functions where most delivery friction actually lives — not on producing more reports. Outcomes are typically measured in predictability of delivery, reduction of leadership-team escalations, and a clearer line of sight from strategy to execution.
