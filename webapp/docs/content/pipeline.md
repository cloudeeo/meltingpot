# News pipeline — topic queue

Bi-weekly cadence. Each entry below is a brief that the `/draft-news` Claude Code command turns into a publishable MDX post under `src/content/posts/`.

**Format for every entry:**

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — "<working title>"

**Scenario.** (Anonymised. Sector, scale, geography. The recognisable shape of the company.)
Series B fintech, ~180 engineers, EMEA. Two product lines, three pods per line, one shared platform team.

**Symptoms.** (What the reader should recognise. 3–5 bullets, each a single sentence.)
- Steering meetings ran 90 minutes and ended without a written decision.
- The EVP of Engineering was the only person tracking dependencies across pods.
- Every roadmap commitment slipped by 30–60% by week 4 of a quarter.
- Same five tickets appeared on every status update for a month.

**Intervention.** (What EF actually did. Operational verbs, no methodology jargon.)
Cut the steering forum from 12 attendees to 5. Introduced a one-page weekly portfolio review with explicit owners per dependency. Re-granted decision rights at pod level for anything not crossing two product lines.

**Outcome.** (The measurable or unambiguously qualitative change.)
Delivery predictability moved from ~50% to ~85% over the following quarter, with no headcount changes. Steering moved from 90-min status meetings to 30-min decision meetings.

**Reader hook for CTA.** (One sentence naming the symptom that triggers self-recognition.)
If steering meetings have become status updates without decisions, that pattern has a name.

**Key insight.** (The broader takeaway — one sentence, used as the closing line of the body.)
Most PMOs fail not because of process but because decision rights were never granted.

**Tags.** (2–4 from canonical list in voice.md.)
governance, pmo, decision-rights

**Mood / image direction.** (Boardroom, lakeside, atrium, late-evening office. See image-prompt.md.)
boardroom
```

## Working agreement

- We promote a brief from "Captured ideas" → fully-fleshed brief → drafted → published.
- A brief that resists fleshing out (you can't anonymise it cleanly, or the outcome was actually mixed) gets killed, not forced.
- Every other Sunday we pick the next brief and draft it via `/draft-news`. Review Monday. Publish Tuesday morning.
- Two posts ahead at all times is the discipline. If the queue drops below two drafted posts, the next session is a queue-building session, not a publishing session.
